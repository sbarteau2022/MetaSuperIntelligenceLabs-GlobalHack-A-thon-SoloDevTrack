## PAMI requirement for the research library

The user requires **PAMI, Phase Augmented Multi Fractal Indexing**, for research retrieval. The repository's N3 SOP names two canonical operations: rank-conditioned phase rotation (`rotate_rank`, rank 0 chunks and rank 1 documents) and a participation-ratio statistic over rank groups (`participation_ratio`, a multi-fractal proxy). `hypermind-nested-kv/src/mapmath.rs` has the local pure-math versions. The current Qdrant research collection stores two 1024D named vectors per **chunk**, with rank1 produced by rotating that same chunk vector. Its receipts establish the phase rotation, but do not by themselves establish document-level rank1 aggregation or participation-ratio admission/query accounting. Do not certify the current dual-rank query as full PAMI until those are wired and measured. Keep PAMI on the Qdrant fact-library path; TPPE remains the separate GlobalMind positional/address contract and must not be substituted for PAMI.

The PAMI promotion gate is: verify the existing rank0 chunk vectors and recorded rotation against stored points; derive rank1 document vectors from all admitted chunks under one versioned aggregation rule; persist parent-child IDs and rank/version payloads; compute participation ratio by rank on the admitted set; run typed rank-aware query and compare recall/latency against the present dual-rank baseline on frozen queries. Migrate to a new collection or versioned named-vector schema with count/hash parity before retiring any existing research points.

No kernel promotion or model-agnostic distillation claim follows from this store repair. Kernel ablations and the typed GlobalMind bridge require separate gates before a live forward-pass claim.
