## Complexity results (2026-11-02, hard line: O(log) or O(1); O(N^2) rejected)
Admitted costs after the rewrite (N = session appends, C = text chars, D = 384):
- Append: O(C + D) — lexical read is Omega(C); TPPE advance is O(1) (eight f64). Sequence cache: rehydrate ONCE per (session, process); no per-append scroll/replay.
- Session of N appends: O(total C). Query: O(C + K log K), K <= 50 bounded. Tail/context: O(K). State memory: O(1) per stream (epoch table of 100 states max, built once).
- REJECTED shapes, measured: old full-carry encode O(pos) per append (64ns@pos0 -> 5,320ns@pos99) = O(N^2)/session. Old rehydrate-per-append O(points-scanned + pos-replayed) per append.
Measured (M5 Pro, release): TPPE advance 34-58 ns/op across pos 0..99 (flat; the 34->58 rise is branch/cache noise, not growth in pos — full-carry at 99 is 5,320). Session of 100 appends: OLD 231.5us vs NEW 4.8us = 48.6x. Encoder throughput vs lexical incumbent: parity at >=1k chars (8,331 vs 8,492 ops/s); raw carry 211k epochs/s (~21M states/s).
Negative (IX): the first epoch-domain bench panicked past 100 steps (radial law fails closed at the boundary — by design; admitted domain is one epoch). (1,2) phase pair is a CONSISTENT state; the fail-closed boundary is negative revolutions / non-finite (test expectation corrected, boundary pinned honestly).
