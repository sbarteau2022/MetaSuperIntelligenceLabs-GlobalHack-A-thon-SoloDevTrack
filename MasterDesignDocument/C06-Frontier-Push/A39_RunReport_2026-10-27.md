## 6. Root-cause analysis (added after second run)

- Treatment always fails at `superposition fail-closed at mlir_op#26 after 3 iterations`.
- `execute_task_in_motion` first tries `discover_and_bridge_novel_concept_trajectories`; on failure it falls to `active_inference_step`, which requires a direct geodesic in the (isolated) carved map. The initial op #26 has no candidate arcs in the scope.
- Control uses the full map/base transformer path and so recovers on some prompts; treatment is strictly more dependent on scope alignment.
- This is an **experiment validity issue**, not a one-line bug: the carved view must contain a usable trajectory near the prompt, or treatment must declare an explicit lawful fallback (base transformer) and record it as a scope change.
- Therefore CC-013 cannot be evaluated as-is. Required fixes: (a) corpus↔vault alignment or pre-ingestion of task trajectories; (b) replace intervention proxy with deterministic decoded-output judge; (c) true tool/retry/token instrumentation; (d) re-run J1–J6.
